Principal Investigator
Dr. T. Heath Ogden
Professor & Chair · Department of Biology · Utah Valley University

Dr. Ogden's research sits at the intersection of insect evolution, phylogenomics, and biology education. He uses molecular and morphological data to build phylogenetic hypotheses for basal pterygotes and Ephemeroptera, then leverages these trees to investigate evolutionary processes — including the origin of flight, evolution of mayfly morphology, and genome diversity.

A parallel thread focuses on pedagogy: flipped classrooms, online vs. face-to-face instruction, and improving evolution acceptance among students.
